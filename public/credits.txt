ASTRA — THE TRIBOAR TRAIL / ASSET & LIBRARY CREDITS

This is an unofficial Dungeons & Dragons-inspired environment prototype.
Dungeons & Dragons, Forgotten Realms, Phandalin, and related setting names
belong to their respective rights holders. No official affiliation is claimed.
The user supplied a Goblin Ambush reference map. Its image is not bundled;
the continuous environment and in-game cartography are recreated in code.

ORIGINAL ENVIRONMENT MATERIALS
The earth, forest-floor, bark, rock, and oak-branch source images in
assets-source/ were AI-generated for this prototype. Optimized WebP textures,
alpha masks, and image-derived normal maps were prepared locally with Sharp.
These are not claimed to be scanned/photogrammetry materials.
Terrain, plants, rocks, character proxy, UI, and map graphics are procedural.

SPRITE FIGURES (PLAYER / HORSES / OXEN)
The 16-direction painted figure cards in public/sprites/ were AI-generated as
4x4 magenta-backed sheets (sources in assets-source/sprites/), then chroma-
keyed, scaled, and packed into atlases locally by scripts/prepare-sprite-
sheets.mjs (npm run assets:sprites, Sharp). Column 0 of each row is the front
view, advancing clockwise through the actor's right side, back, and left; the
engine picks the column nearest the camera's viewing angle and crossfades
between neighbours. Goblin enemies use the procedural 3D rigs until their
sheets are regenerated.

CHAPTER I ADDITIONS
The Narrator is an AI-generated spoken performance in the voice auditioned
and selected for this session. All six local MP3 passages use the user's
provided story text. No API key is required for playback.
The optional Gemini regeneration script uses gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview,
a private environment key, natural-language direction, and inline audio tags.
It is not run automatically or called from browser code.

Additional original AI-generated texture sources: wagon oak, flax sackcloth,
and short animal coat. Normal maps are derived, not photogrammetry scans.
The wagon, cargo containers, casks, sacks, tools, lanterns, yoke, reins, and
hinged lids are procedural models created for this chapter.
The animal surfaces are derived from the credited Horse.glb, reshaped into
standing horse/ox bodies, subdivided, textured, and given new skeletal rigs
for walking, breathing, head lowering, looking, and tail movement.
Loop subdivision uses three-subdivide — Stevinz — MIT:
licenses/three-subdivide-MIT.txt
