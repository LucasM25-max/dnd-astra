ASTRA — THE TRIBOAR TRAIL / ASSET & LIBRARY CREDITS

This is an unofficial Dungeons & Dragons-inspired environment prototype.
Dungeons & Dragons, Forgotten Realms, Phandalin, and related setting names
belong to their respective rights holders. No official affiliation is claimed.
The user supplied a Goblin Ambush reference map. Its image is not bundled;
the continuous environment and in-game cartography are recreated in code.

ORIGINAL ENVIRONMENT MATERIALS
The earth-path, forest-soil (forest floor), leaf-litter, mossy-bark, rock,
and oak-branch source images in assets-source/ were AI-generated for this
prototype.
Optimized WebP textures, alpha masks, and image-derived normal maps were
prepared locally with Sharp. These are not claimed to be scanned/photogrammetry
materials. Terrain, plants, rocks, character proxy, UI, and map graphics are
procedural.

CHARACTERS & ANIMALS
The Wanderer, the yoked oxen, and the horses are original procedurally
painted canvas sprite sheets (sixteen direction variants per actor, with
idle, walk, sprint, seated, and head-down animation frames), displayed on
upright billboards that crossfade between neighbouring directions. No
external character meshes, rigs, or image-generated sprite sheets are used.

CHAPTER I ADDITIONS
The Narrator is an AI-generated spoken performance in the voice auditioned
and selected for this session. All six local MP3 passages use the user's
provided story text. No API key is required for playback.
The optional Gemini regeneration script uses gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview,
a private environment key, natural-language direction, and inline audio tags.
It is not run automatically or called from browser code.

Additional original AI-generated texture sources: wagon oak, flax sackcloth,
short animal coat, the ivory dice-body PBR set, the dragon dice tray, and the
campfire ember/smoke sprites. Normal maps are derived, not photogrammetry
scans. The dice and particle WebP files are size-optimized conversions of
those sources (scripts/prepare-system-assets.mjs); the dust-mote sprite is a
procedural soft dot after its source shipped with a baked-in artefact.
The wagon, cargo containers, casks, sacks, tools, lanterns, yoke, reins, and
hinged lids are procedural models created for this chapter.
The character and animal sprites are painted in code (canvas 2D) as
sixteen-direction billboards covering walking, breathing, head lowering,
looking, and tail movement; no external sprite art is bundled.
